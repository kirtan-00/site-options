About Fitkit Pro

I had a fitness band and an app that forgot me between conversations. So I took the hardware and shaped the software around it.

Fitkit pulls the band's heart, sleep and movement data every morning, works out readiness, strain and sleep need, and keeps a coach that remembers what happened last week. Two people use it, each in their own walled-off copy.

How it was built

What it is
A personal WHOOP-style tracker for a Fitbit Air: sleep, readiness, strain, habits, and a coach that remembers
Why I built it
The Fitbit coach forgets everything between chats. I wanted memory and the real cost of each habit in my own numbers
Privacy
Each person gets their own isolated instance, and backups are encrypted on my Mac
Screenshots
Test data only. Any screen shown runs on made-up numbers, never anyone's real readings
