About Vibe Raas Tickets

Ohm and I brought back The Vibe Tribe, his event company, for Navratri 2026: Vibe Raas, three nights of garba at Tathastu Party Plot, Ognaj, 17 to 19 October. A revived company has no systems, so I built them.

Passes were sold by a team of sellers, not a website, so the till is a Telegram bot. A seller types the sale the way they would say it, checks the preview, taps Confirm, and the bot sends back a QR pass for the buyer.

At the pickup points, any phone becomes a scanner. Green means hand over, amber means part of the order is already taken, red means stop: it has all been collected, and the screen says where and when. It works with no signal, and several phones can scan at once without handing out the same pass twice.
