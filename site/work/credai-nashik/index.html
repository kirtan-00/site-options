About Shelter Property Expo 2026

Shelter is CREDAI Nashik Metro's property expo, and 2026 is its 12th edition: five days at Dongre Ground on Gangapur Road. CrayWingz handles the expo's marketing, design and website. The look of this site came from the project's designer, as a single HTML page. I rebuilt that page in Astro and added what it didn't have.

The main addition is the ground plan. I took the organisers' printed layout and put an interactive layer on top, so you can zoom into any of the four domes and point at a stall to see its category and size. I also built the postcard hero from the Figma frame and an enquiry form made to send leads to a Google Sheet.
