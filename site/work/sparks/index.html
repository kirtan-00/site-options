Sparks

A free browser app for DOPs and gaffers to plan the lighting on a photo of the location, with the numbers to back it up.

Open in the full siteOpen Sparks

About Sparks

You upload a photo of the location and Sparks estimates the depth on your device. Then you place real light fixtures in the scene.

It gives you the numbers: the light level at the subject, each light's ratio to the key in stops, whether a fixture can reach that from where it stands, and the total amps for the truck list. It runs fully on your device, with no upload and no account, and it is free.

This was an experimental build.

Behind the scenes
