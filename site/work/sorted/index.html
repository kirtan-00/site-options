About sorted

sorted is a Mac app that reads the whole disk and lets you search it in plain words. Type "fishing boats" and the boats come up, photos and 4K clips in the same grid. Drop in one photo of a person and it finds every other frame of them. Then it hands you folders.

Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere for it to upload to.

sorted started with a shoot in Diu for CrayWingz. Over 5 days we shot a documentary, more than an hour of podcast and over 30 testimonials, plus a lot of photos, and came home with 3 TB. I built the first version to sort that pile for our designers, and for the editors, because there were several videos to cut. It helped the designers a little. Then I built an editing module. On a later product shoot for Dr. Naturals we felt the need for product photo sorting and for something that sorts while you shoot, so I built product sorting and the Live module.
