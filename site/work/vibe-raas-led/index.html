About Vibe Raas LED

Ohm and I brought back The Vibe Tribe, his event company, for Navratri 2026: Vibe Raas, a garba night at Tathastu Party Plot, Ognaj. A revived company has no systems, so I built them.

Three LED walls: a 50 ft stage screen and two side screens. We wanted them to move with the music, not loop a video, so I wrote the software. It listens to the mixer and drives eight hand-built visuals drawn from Gujarati textile and folk motifs: Tunnel, Stripes, Mandala, Ghoomar, Patola, Jali, Damru and Shiv.

Behind the scenes

Anything that looked like a stock garba graphic died in review. The visuals that worked were pure pattern and colour pushed on the beat, and they looked best on the small side screens.

How it was built

Screens
One big stage LED and two side LEDs, each told what to play from my phone
Controls
Pick the visual, the colour set (Kumkum, Haldi, Mor, Raat), the centre logo, tap tempo, intensity
Backup plan
A single offline file on the laptop in case the venue internet died
What got cut
Dancer cutouts, clip-art diyas and dandiyas, AI-looking dot transitions, a drawn Nataraja figure
