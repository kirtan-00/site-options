The work for Zepto

Zepto delivers groceries in ten minutes, so the content can't take ten days. I produce their monthly reels and handle the client communication, then get each one made with a team or by myself. I shoot and edit them too.

Most months they send the concept. Some months the concept is mine. Either way it comes back shot, cut and ready to post while the joke is still fresh. I'm also in frame, as a creator on Zepto's own page.
